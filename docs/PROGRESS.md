Knowledge base and inline suggestions, phases 2–5 (Nov 4, per `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-knowledge-base-inline-suggestions.md`): a note now reaches a classmate as a *suggestion*, not a raw fragment. `knowledge_entries` groups the notes on a slide into points of knowledge with an AI summary (a single source is kept verbatim), `entry_sources` tracks which notes feed which point, `lecture_topics` names 5–15 topics per lecture, and `page_gaps` records where a page is missing something. The merge-and-check job runs when a student leaves a notebook (`POST /api/notebooks/:id/close`), costs one call per changed slide (a source hash skips unchanged ones), and is swept every 10 minutes; a reported point is rechecked immediately and stays hidden until then. The page-indexing call also finds gaps, and each is offered in the order peer → slide → AI. On the client the Classmates/Explain/Quiz panel is replaced by quiet margin markers, a card that opens in place at the gap (Add / Dismiss / Not helpful / Looks wrong), a lasso popover with Explain / Example / Why? and a typed question, and a slim suggestions panel that also lists the author's own notes as shared, private (with reason) or disputed (with the AI's explanation). Sharing routes and controls are gone by user decision; `withheld` stays dormant. Prompts carry the knowledge base as `[E<id>]` with server-side citation validation. 22 unit/API tests, 10 browser scenarios.

Note-writing and suggestions (Nov 4, user request: the writing experience and the suggestions still felt unnatural). Found by walking the app rather than reading it: **adding a classmate's or the slide's suggestion never saved** (the client sent `source`, the server required `basis`, and entry citations were rejected by the schema) and only looked saved because the local render kept it; the e2e assertion had never reloaded. A notebook was made only from a long-press menu, the paper choice applied to page 1 only (every later page silently became ruled), there was no memo, and typed text was a fixed-width box laid over the ruling. Now: `New notebook` is a button with three labelled paper cards (Lined, Grid, Plain) and each notebook records the paper its new pages use; one sheet can be re-papered without losing content; typed text starts in the writing column, snaps to a rule and grows downward. **Memo is an object, not a page kind** (user correction): a sticky note stuck anywhere on a page, four tints, draggable, resizable, typed in place, and drawn into the page render so it is indexed and shareable like ink. Item schema now `source: peer|slide|ai` on both sides.

Suggestions went through two rounds of user direction. First "a card beside the page"; then, after the user pointed at how Copilot works in an editor, **inline**: the suggestion is written faintly into the page at the gap with a `＋` in the margin, accepted in one click (or Enter) and merged as a quoted note below the student's writing; the page is the record, so undo brings the suggestion back, while Escape/"Not now" is temporary and Dismiss/"Don't suggest this again" is remembered. The ghost takes no pointer events over the writing area (the lasso test caught the first version swallowing strokes). Second, the user asked for a browser: the panel now has **This page** and **Whole notebook** tabs, the latter listing every suggestion grouped by page in notebook order, each expandable to the suggestion plus a crop of the classmate's actual handwriting when it comes from one note, with "Add to that page" and "Go to page". `Ask` became a first-class header button with a per-page ask box; the dead "Quiz me" button was removed.

Known limits / next:
- Text boxes have no resize handle; images cannot be resized after insertion; no shape tool or page templates beyond ruled/blank/grid.
- Bounding boxes from the model are approximate; crops occasionally include neighboring lines.
- Explain/quiz latency depends on the provider (seconds to a minute); no streaming yet.
- No human evaluation of transcription accuracy or usefulness yet.
- Named sharing: no revoke and no Forward action; a copy can be detached to a snapshot. Co-editing is poll-based, not real-time (no cursors/WebSocket/CRDT); simultaneous typing in one text box is last-write-wins. A live session has no automatic timeout if the host disappears. Group notes and professor notebooks stay out of the AI bank.
- The handwriting improver is a deterministic vector-ink render; a learned enhancement model or hosted OCR fallback is not wired in.
- The systemd unit and Cloudflare hostname from v1 (`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`) were left untouched; restart the service to serve v2 (`npm run build` first).
